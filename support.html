Contact

Found a bug, a level that looks wrong, or have an idea? Write to cucerenco.m23@gmail.com. Please mention your device and the level number.

Common questions

How do I play? Tap a train and it drives along its track. If another train is in the way it bumps into it and you lose a heart. Find the order that lets every train out.

I’m stuck. Hint makes the trains glow that can leave right now, and Crane lifts any one train out. Both are limited per level.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. Deleting the app starts you again from level 1.

Why do I see no ads? Where the law asks for your consent and it was not given, the game shows no ads at all.
